About the App

Joe's Speed Monitor uses your Android device's GPS/GNSS location data to provide live vehicle speed and performance measurements.

It is designed for automotive enthusiasts who want a simple way to monitor speed and record performance runs using their phone or tablet.

Features

  • Live GPS speed
  • Top-speed recording
  • Acceleration timing
  • 1/8-mile elapsed time
  • 1/8-mile trap speed
  • 1/4-mile elapsed time
  • 1/4-mile trap speed
