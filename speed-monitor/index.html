About the App

Joe’s Speed Monitor is a simple, low-cost motorsports timing app built for Android.

Choose between two dedicated modes:

Drag Racing

Measure vehicle speed and acceleration performance using your Android device's built-in GPS/GNSS or a compatible external 25hz USB GPS receiver.

  • Live vehicle speed
  • Top-speed recording
  • 0–60 mph timing
  • 0–100 km/h timing
  • 1/8-mile elapsed time and trap speed
  • 1/4-mile elapsed time and trap speed
  • Detailed speed graph
  • Save and review completed performance runs

Circuit

Connect a compatible external USB GPS receiver and turn Joe's Speed Monitor into a circuit mapping and automatic lap-timing system.

Drive a mapping lap to record the circuit and establish the start/finish line. After the circuit has been mapped, Joe's Speed Monitor can automatically detect start/finish crossings and record your lap times.

  • GPS circuit mapping
  • Automatic lap timing
  • Current lap time
  • Last lap time
  • Best lap time
  • Lap history
  • Live circuit display
  • Start Mapping and Finish Mapping buttons
  • Hands-free voice commands for Start Mapping and Finish Mapping

Circuit mode requires a compatible external USB GPS receiver for the faster GPS update rate used for circuit mapping and start/finish detection.

Joe’s Speed Monitor was created for enthusiasts who want useful motorsports performance data without expensive dedicated timing equipment.

Whether you are measuring acceleration, recording quarter-mile runs, checking top speed, or timing laps at a circuit, Joe's Speed Monitor puts the essential tools directly on your Android device.

Circuit Mode

Circuit mode is designed to make lap timing simple. Connect a compatible external USB GPS receiver, enter the circuit name, and perform one mapping lap.

1. Connect External GPS

Connect your compatible USB GPS receiver and wait for Joe's Speed Monitor to confirm that the external GPS is connected and has a valid satellite fix.

2. Start Mapping

As you cross your chosen start/finish line, press Start Mapping or use the hands-free voice command:

“Start Mapping”

3. Drive the Circuit

Drive one complete lap while Joe's Speed Monitor records GPS points and builds a live map of the circuit.

4. Finish Mapping

When you return to the start/finish line, press Finish Mapping or use the voice command:

“Finish Mapping”

5. Automatic Lap Timing

After the circuit is mapped, Joe's Speed Monitor can automatically detect future start/finish crossings and track your current lap, previous lap, best lap and lap history.

Voice control allows the mapping process to be operated without reaching for the screen while driving.

Compatible External GPS Receiver

QUESCAN Android USB Type-C 25 Hz GPS Receiver

Joe's Speed Monitor supports the QUESCAN Android USB Type-C 25 Hz GPS receiver for faster and more frequent GPS updates than typical built-in phone GPS receivers.

The external receiver connects directly to a compatible Android phone or tablet using USB Type-C.

When the receiver is connected and USB permission is granted, Joe's Speed Monitor automatically detects the receiver and can use it as the active GPS source.

The external GPS receiver can be used in Drag Racing mode and is required for Circuit mode.

  • Up to 25 GPS updates per second
  • USB Type-C connection
  • Automatically detected by Joe's Speed Monitor
  • Live vehicle speed measurement
  • 0–60 mph acceleration timing
  • 0–100 km/h acceleration timing
  • 1/8-mile elapsed time and trap speed
  • 1/4-mile elapsed time and trap speed
  • Circuit mapping
  • Automatic circuit lap timing
  • Start/finish line detection
  • Android phone GPS/GNSS remains available for Drag Racing when the external receiver is not connected

Recommended for Drag Racing mode and required for Circuit mode.

GPS Accuracy

GPS-based performance measurements are affected by receiver quality, satellite reception, update rate, antenna placement, vehicle speed and environmental conditions.

A higher-frequency external GPS receiver provides more frequent position and speed updates than typical phone GPS/GNSS hardware, which can improve the resolution of acceleration and lap-timing measurements.

Joe's Speed Monitor displays the detected external GPS update rate when supported by the connected receiver.
